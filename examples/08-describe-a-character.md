# 08 · Describe a character or object without names

**Request:** "Describe this character from the screenshot for a prompt, without saying who it is."

Order: age and build → face → hair → clothing top to bottom → pose → expression → setting and light.

```
An original fictional character, not resembling any real actor or celebrity: a tall, lanky seventeen-year-old boy with a long face densely covered in freckles, light blue eyes and a slightly crooked nose. Shaggy copper-ginger hair with a heavy fringe falling into his eyes. He wears a slightly oversized dark charcoal V-neck school sweater with burgundy-and-gold trim, a rumpled cream shirt with an uneven collar, a loosely knotted burgundy-and-gold striped tie and dark grey trousers. No badges or crests. He stands with his arms crossed low, shoulders hunched, with an awkward, sheepish half-smile, in a candle-lit stone corridor.
```

For an object: era and type → body colours → front → profile → rear → wear.

```
A small vintage 1960s two-door compact saloon: a pale powder-blue body with a cream-white roof and a thin cream side stripe; round chrome-ringed headlights set wide apart and a broad chrome grille of fine horizontal bars; a boxy upright roofline with a rear window raked inward and small sharp tail fins; chrome hubcaps; worn, slightly faded paint with small dents. No brand badges; the licence plate is unreadable.
```

**Note for the user:** the names, brand and plate text were left out on purpose. The traits that make it recognisable are the freckles with the shaggy copper fringe, and the inward-raked rear window with the two-tone paint. Strengthen those if the model drifts.
