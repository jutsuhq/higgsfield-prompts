# 01 · Idea to an 8-second clip

**Request:** "A night-shift baker at work, something cosy, for TikTok."

**Settings:** text-to-video, 9:16, 8 s.

```
A night-shift baker dusts flour over a slab of dough, folds it twice with the heel of her hands, and slides a tray of braided loaves into a glowing brick oven, in a tiny bakery kitchen at 3 a.m. The street outside the steamed-up window is empty and blue, and the kitchen is warm and cluttered with proofing baskets.
The visuals feature warm tungsten light against cold blue night, floating flour dust caught in the light, soft film grain and shallow depth of field.
The camera starts on an extreme close-up of her floury hands, pulls back to a medium shot as she folds the dough, then follows the tray to the oven and pushes in on the loaves as the oven door closes.
Audio: <flour patters onto the wooden bench> <the dough thumps softly as it is folded> <the heavy iron oven door creaks shut>, under a low hum of the oven fan. No music, no dialogue.
```

**Why it works**

- The action has a start and an end (dust → fold → into the oven). "Bakes bread" would give a static shot.
- The camera is a sequence of three moves, each triggered by the action.
- Each sound event is tied to the action that makes it, and silence is stated explicitly.
- Duration and ratio stay in the UI, not in the prompt.
