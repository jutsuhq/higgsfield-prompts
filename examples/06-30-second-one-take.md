# 06 · Tell a 30-second story in one take

**Request:** "A 30-second ad-style story: a tiny ramen shop opening for the night."

**Settings:** text-to-video, 21:9, 30 s.

```
[Generation Goal] A 30-second one-take story about an old ramen cook opening his tiny shop for the night, told in four stages with one main change per stage.

[Stage 1] Setup: at dusk, the cook rolls up the metal shutter of a narrow ramen counter in a quiet alley and switches on the paper lantern. End state: the lantern glows, eight empty stools, steam rising from the stockpot.
[Stage 2] Continue from the previous stage: the first customer, a tired office worker in a grey suit, ducks under the curtain and sits; the cook ladles broth and arranges toppings. End state: a steaming bowl in front of the worker, who breathes in the smell.
[Stage 3] Turn: rain starts; three more customers hurry in, shaking umbrellas, and the counter fills with chatter and steam. End state: four customers eating side by side, rain falling behind them.
[Stage 4] Resolution: the cook wipes the counter and glances at the full row with a quiet, satisfied smile as the camera drifts back out to the alley. End state: the glowing shop small in the rainy alley, mid-motion as a fifth customer approaches.

The camera flows as one continuous take: it starts wide at the end of the alley, glides in along the counter, settles behind the cook's shoulder while he serves, and finally drifts back out into the rain.
The visuals feature warm lantern and tungsten light against blue dusk, rising steam, wet reflections in the later stages, and gentle film grain.
Audio: (a soft lo-fi guitar loop that grows warmer as the shop fills and settles on a final chord) <the shutter rattles up> <broth pours into a ceramic bowl> <rain patters on the awning>. Customers murmur, but no lines are spoken.
[Maintain Consistency] Keep the cook's identity, white headband and navy apron, the eight stools, the alley layout and the rain direction consistent throughout. One continuous take, no cuts.
```

**Why it works**

- There are four stages and four changes (opens → first customer → rain and crowd → satisfaction), each with an end state that carries continuity into the next.
- The camera has one through-line across all stages, and the music has an arc.
- It ends mid-motion, so the clip can later be extended.
